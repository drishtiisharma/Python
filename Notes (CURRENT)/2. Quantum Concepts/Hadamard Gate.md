
# Hadamard Gate

The Hadamard gate is a physical operation performed on a quantum bit (qubit).

## What It Does

- It takes a clear, definite state—either a $\lvert0\rangle$ or a $\lvert1\rangle$.
- It changes that qubit into a mathematical combination of both $\lvert0\rangle$ and $\lvert1\rangle$ at the exact same time. This state is called a superposition.

## The Exact Result

When a qubit passes through this gate, its properties are altered so that:

- It has a 50% probability of being measured as a `0`.
- It has a 50% probability of being measured as a `1`.

In quantum computing, this operation is essential because it moves a qubit out of a single classical value and prepares it to hold multiple data values simultaneously for complex calculations.
