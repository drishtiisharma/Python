# Intro to Number Theory

Number theory is the branch of mathematics that studies:

- properties and relationships of integers
- particularly prime numbers
- divisibility, modular arithmetic
- diophantine equations

It explores fundamental questions about whole numbers, such as which numbers are prime, how numbers can be factored, and what patterns emerge in arithmetic operations.

# Why study Number Theory for Cryptography

Modern cryptography uses math problems that are fast to solve one way but very slow to reverse. 

For Example, RSA encryption works because multiplying two large prime numbers is easy, but finding those original primes from the result is hard. Elliptic curve cryptography works because performing calculations on specific curve points is straightforward, but reversing those calculations is difficult. These methods rely on basic number properties like remainders, powers, and prime factors to keep data secure.
